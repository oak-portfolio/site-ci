Oak is an AI product engineer who designs, builds and ships agent products from the first sketch to the last release note, and who treats every launch as a chance to cut what does not earn its place. The work spans a portfolio site that answers questions in plain language, a worker that grounds each reply in recorded facts, a set of evaluation suites that catch regressions before visitors do, and a design system that keeps every surface calm, dark and consistent. Along the way the same habits repeat: write the test first, keep the change small, measure the result, and say plainly what was not checked.
